Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This notice describes the information PALLAVA Water Tank Cleaning Service may receive when you contact us or use the enquiry features on this website. It applies to information you choose to provide to us.

Details you may choose to share

Depending on how you contact us, the information may include:

  • your name and phone number;
  • the service you are asking about, your area and preferred service date;
  • any explanation or additional message you enter in the enquiry form;
  • the contents of a WhatsApp message or an email you send us.

You can also contact us by telephone without using the website form. Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or other information that is not needed to discuss your service request.

Why we use enquiry information

We use the details you provide to understand the work you are requesting, respond to questions, discuss availability or pricing, arrange a visit and communicate about the service. We may also refer to earlier enquiry details when you contact us again about the same request.

Form processing and external platforms

The website registration form sends the details you submit to a Google Sheets document through a Google Apps Script web app. The submitted details are stored in the PALLAVA customer-registration spreadsheet, and a notification email is sent to the service contact address. Google services process the information as part of this workflow.

If you choose to call, email or message us through WhatsApp, those communications are handled using the relevant telephone, email or messaging provider. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. This website also loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which may result in your browser requesting font files from Google.

Access, storage and sharing

Enquiry details are used by the people handling PALLAVA's customer requests and service arrangements. We do not offer customer enquiry details for sale. Information may remain in communication histories or business records for as long as it is reasonably needed to respond to the request, complete related work and maintain appropriate records.

Your requests

If you want to check, correct or request deletion of information you have sent us, contact us using the details below. We may need enough information to identify the relevant enquiry before acting on a request, and some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Updates to this notice

We may revise this page when our website, enquiry process or the way we handle information changes. The date shown above indicates when this notice was last reviewed.
