About

Focused Care for Water Storage and Heating Systems

Our work, in practical terms

PALLAVA Water Tank Cleaning Service is a service provider for customers who need attention to the places and equipment used to store or heat water. The work we take up is specific: cleaning tanks and sumps, maintaining water-heating equipment, and applying waterproof coating inside suitable sump areas.

Every site is different. The size and condition of the tank, access to the work area and the type of equipment all affect how a job is carried out. We discuss the requirement before arranging the service so that the requested work is clear.

Services available from PALLAVA

  • Water Tank Cleaning – cleaning the inside of overhead water storage tanks and removing accumulated dirt, sludge, algae and sediment from the tank surfaces.
  • Underground Sump Cleaning – cleaning underground water storage sumps by removing settled mud, silt, dirt and other accumulated residue.
  • Underground Sump Waterproof Painting – preparing suitable underground sump surfaces and applying a suitable waterproof coating to help reduce water seepage. The required treatment depends on the sump condition.
  • Electric Geyser Service – inspection, cleaning and servicing of electric geysers. The work carried out depends on the geyser model, condition and accessible components.
  • Solar Water Heater Tube Cleaning – cleaning the evacuated glass tubes of solar water heaters and removing accumulated dirt and deposits from the tubes. This service is for solar water heater tubes, not solar panels.

How to arrange a service

Tell us which tank or equipment needs attention, where the property is located and what issue you have noticed. We can then discuss the requirement and the next steps with you. For safety and access reasons, the work details are confirmed according to the actual site and equipment.
