What We Offer

Services We Provide

We provide five focused services: overhead water tank cleaning, underground sump cleaning, underground sump waterproof painting, electric geyser servicing, and solar water heater evacuated glass tube cleaning.

01

Water Tank Cleaning

We clean the inside of overhead water storage tanks and remove accumulated dirt, sludge, algae and sediment from the tank surfaces.

Register Now 02

Underground Sump Cleaning

We clean underground water storage sumps by removing settled mud, silt, dirt and other accumulated residue from the sump.

Register Now 03

Solar Water Heater Tube Cleaning

We clean the evacuated glass tubes of solar water heaters, removing accumulated dirt and deposits from the tubes. This service is for solar water heater tubes, not solar panels.

Register Now 04

Electric Geyser Service

We inspect, clean and service electric geysers. The work carried out depends on the geyser model, condition and accessible components.

Register Now 05

Underground Sump Waterproof Painting

We prepare suitable underground sump surfaces and apply a suitable waterproof coating to help reduce water seepage. The required treatment depends on the sump condition.
